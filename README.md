# OMM 1 Written Exam 1 · Practice Questions

A phone-friendly question book of every practice question collected for OMM 1 Written Exam #1 (Weeks 1–8): in-class polls, clickers and Canvas quizzes, TA exam reviews and textbook chapter questions, merged across years (655 questions), with answers, explanations and the lecture slide behind each one.

Open it at https://yoelplutchok.github.io/omm-1-written-1/

Personal study aid. TA-written questions are not an official answer key.

## Cardio-Pulm 1 · Multiple Choice

`cardio-pulm/` is the Cardio-Pulm 1 flashcard deck (2,127 cards) turned into multiple-choice questions on one scrolling page, in the same format. Each card's answer is mixed in with three wrong choices. To rebuild it after editing the cards or choices in `cardio-pulm/src/`, run `python3 cardio-pulm/src/build.py`.
